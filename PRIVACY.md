# Privacy Policy for FlipMargin

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

FlipMargin ("the Extension") is developed and maintained by Robert Allen (Treborx Software). We respect your privacy and designed FlipMargin to operate locally inside your browser.

## 1. Data Processed Locally in Your Browser
All eBay fee calculations, profit margin badges, and CSV spreadsheet exports are processed 100% locally within your browser tab. FlipMargin does not transmit, log, or store the eBay searches you perform or the CSV files you export on any external server.

## 2. Subscription & License Verification (ExtensionPay & Stripe)
If you choose to upgrade to FlipMargin Pro, payments and license activation are processed securely via **ExtensionPay** (https://extensionpay.com) and **Stripe** (https://stripe.com).
- **Email Address & License Key:** ExtensionPay collects your email address solely to link your paid subscription or lifetime license to your browser via `chrome.storage` and to allow you to restore your Pro license across devices.
- **Payment Details:** All credit/debit card details are handled directly by Stripe. FlipMargin never sees, touches, or stores your payment card numbers.

## 3. Limited Use Disclosure
The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties for advertising, creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to providing the Extension's single purpose.

## 4. Contact
For support, refund requests, or privacy questions, please contact us via https://github.com/treborx4/flipmargin/issues.
