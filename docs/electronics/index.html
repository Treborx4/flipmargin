Electronics fees on eBay

Most consumer electronics use the standard 13.6% rate on the first $7,500, plus the per-order fee. Some tech subcategories have had different rates, so it's worth a quick look at eBay's fee page for the exact category you're listing in. Use Custom in the dropdown if yours is different.

Promoted listings and electronics

Electronics categories are crowded and a lot of sellers pay for promoted listings to get seen. That ad rate is charged on the full sale, just like the final value fee. Open "More options" in the calculator and add your ad rate so you don't get surprised.

Before you flip electronics

  • Check that phones aren't carrier locked or blacklisted, and that laptops aren't locked to an account.
  • Insure anything expensive when you ship it, and add that to your label cost.
  • Factor in returns. A target ROI of 25% to 30% leaves some room for one going wrong.
