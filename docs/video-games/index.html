Video game fees on eBay

Most video games, accessories and consoles use the standard 13.6% rate plus the per-order fee. A few subcategories have had their own rates over the years, so if your listing shows something different on eBay's fee page, pick Custom and type it in.

Shipping games without losing money

A single disc game in a case usually ships well under a pound, and Ground Advantage is the normal pick. Consoles are heavier and often land in a different price range for labels, so weigh it before you list. If you offer free shipping, raise the price to cover it and put the label cost in the calculator so the payout is right.

Flipping game lots

  • Price the good titles one by one, then decide what the lot is worth to you with the max buy number.
  • Cartridge only games sell for less than complete in box ones, so look at sold listings for the same condition.
  • Test consoles before you list. Returns on "untested" consoles get expensive fast.
