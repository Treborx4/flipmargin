How the 2026 eBay fees work

For most categories eBay takes a final value fee of 13.6% of the total the buyer pays. That total includes shipping and any sales tax, which surprises a lot of people. On top of that there's a per-order fee: $0.30 if the order is $10.00 or less, $0.40 if it's over $10.

If you pay for a Basic Store or higher, most categories drop to around 12.7%. Pick that from the fee dropdown if it applies to you. The rate only covers the first $7,500 of a sale. Anything above that gets charged 2.35%, and the calculator handles that for you.

What "max buy" means

Max buy is the most you can pay for an item and still make your target return after fees and shipping. With the default 30% ROI, if you'd net $65 after everything, your max buy is $50 ($50 plus 30% is $65). Change the ROI box if you work with a different number. A lot of people use 30% to 50% for small stuff and less for expensive items that sell fast.

Shipping, the part people forget

Put what the buyer pays for shipping in "Shipping charged" and what the label actually costs you in "Your label cost". Free shipping listings are just $0 charged. eBay still charges its percentage on whatever shipping the buyer pays, so charging $8 shipping on a $5 label doesn't make you a full $3.
