Trading card fees on eBay

Individual cards in trading card categories (sports, Pokemon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh, One Piece and so on) have been charged a slightly lower rate than most categories, 13.25% on the first $7,500. Sealed product and supplies can fall under different categories, so check where you're listing. If eBay's fee page shows a different rate for your category, pick Custom and type it in.

Why cheap cards are rough

On a $1 card the per-order fee alone is 30 cents. Add the percentage fee and a stamp and envelope, and you can sell a card and make nothing. Try a $0.99 price with $0.97 shipping cost in the calculator and you'll see it. That's why a lot of sellers bundle low cards into lots or set a minimum like $2.99 plus shipping.

Tips for card flippers

  • Use the eBay standard envelope or a plain white envelope for cheap singles, and a bubble mailer with tracking for anything you'd be upset to lose.
  • Look at sold listings, not active ones, when you set the sale price in the calculator.
  • For graded cards, include what you paid for grading in your item cost so the ROI is honest.
